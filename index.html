HISTORY

Journey Through Indian Civilization

Select an era to explore important civilizations, rulers, monuments and achievements.

Ancient
c. 2600–1900 BCE

Indus Valley Civilization

Famous for planned cities, drainage systems, brick architecture and the Great Bath.

Ancient
c. 322–185 BCE

Maurya Empire

A major ancient Indian empire associated with Chandragupta Maurya and Emperor Ashoka.

Ancient
4th–6th century CE

Gupta Period

Known for achievements in mathematics, astronomy, literature, sculpture and architecture.

Medieval
9th–13th century CE

Chola Power

Remembered for temple architecture, bronze sculpture, administration and maritime influence.

Medieval
1206–1526 CE

Delhi Sultanate

A series of dynasties that ruled large parts of the Indian subcontinent.

Medieval
1336–1646 CE

Vijayanagara Empire

A powerful South Indian empire famous for Hampi, temples, markets and cultural achievements.

Medieval
1526–1857 CE

Mughal Empire

Known for architecture, gardens, miniature painting, administration and cultural synthesis.

Medieval
17th–19th century

Maratha Power

Remembered for Shivaji Maharaj, fort architecture, administration and the Maratha Confederacy.

Modern
1857–Present

Modern India

Explore India's freedom movement, independence, constitution and journey as a republic.

CULTURE

India's Creative Heritage

Discover traditional art, architecture and crafts from different regions of India.

🎨

Madhubani Painting

A traditional painting style associated with Bihar, known for vibrant colors and detailed patterns.

🛕

South Indian Temple Art

Explore magnificent temples, sculptures, gopurams and stone craftsmanship.

🎨

Warli Art

A tribal art tradition using simple geometric forms to depict everyday life.

🧵

Kashmiri Crafts

Famous for carpets, embroidery, papier-mâché and detailed handicrafts.

🖌️

Gond Art

A tribal art tradition known for patterned depictions of animals, nature and stories.

🌿

Northeastern Traditions

Discover indigenous textiles, crafts, festivals and cultural traditions of Northeast India.
